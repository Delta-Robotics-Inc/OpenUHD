Stand-in assumptions

  • motor figures interpolate linearly between the 10 rows of the maker's table (0 at 0 % throttle); pack current = table electrical power ÷ 22.2 V nominal
  • maker's table used the MEPS SZ4942 prop at 25.2 V, not the fitted propeller
  • avionics: DJI O4 minimum supply power 10 W, GNSS 50 mA at 5 V, FC + receiver 2.5 W (assumed), BEC efficiency 85 % (assumed)
  • all-up weight = 584 g from systemMass (derived:mass.all_up, of which 151.2 g is CAD volume × assumed material) + 20 g assumed for the 4 parts with no mass in the model (FC/ESC SH1.0 8-pin cable, Panasonic EEU-FR1V471 470 μF 35 V low-ESR electrolytic capacitor, XT60 battery lead (12 AWG), DJI O4 3-in-1 cable)
  • usable capacity 80 % of rated
  • thermal resistance 2 K/W and capacity 20 J/K (board in prop wash) — not in the model
  • effective conduction resistance 4 m ohm per channel and 1.5 W switching loss per channel at full throttle — not in the model
  • starting 8 °C above ambient after the previous flight segment
  • generic LiPo open-circuit voltage curve
  • internal resistance 4 m ohm per cell — not in the model
