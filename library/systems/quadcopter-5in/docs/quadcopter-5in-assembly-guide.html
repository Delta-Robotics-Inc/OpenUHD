Install the air unit and camera

M2 x 18 mm SHCS

M2 nyloc nut, DIN 985, A2 stainless
  1. Insert 2 × M2 x 5 mm camera screw through the camera side plates from one side.
  2. Seat the DJI O4 Air Unit onto the camera side plates.
  3. Insert 2 × M2 x 5 mm camera screw from the opposite side.
  4. Insert 4 × M2 x 18 mm SHCS from below, up through the transmission module mount.
  5. Slide 4 × M2 nylon spacer, Ø2.7/4.0 x 3 mm over the screws.
  6. Lower the DJI O4 Air Unit onto the transmission module mount (3 mm gap).
  7. Finish with 4 × M2 nyloc nut, DIN 985, A2 stainless; tighten until snug.
Torque —— not in modelStep 5 of 11
CAUTION Needs airflow. Install inside the frame in a well-ventilated spot (near air intake/exhaust), within ~1 cm of the propellers to use downwash, and not in an enclosed area; a heat-conductive pad to the carbon frame extends run time. Ground standby from cold start about 2.5 min at 25 C (ground low-power mode on); above 25 C ambient use external cooling. — dji-o4-air-unit
